# Customer-Churn-Analysis
End-to-end *Customer Churn Analysis* in *Power BI* uncovering key churn drivers (month-to-month contracts, early tenure, fiber optic &amp; electronic check users) with strategic recommendations to reduce revenue loss.

## Key Metrics (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 7,043
* **Churned Customers:** 1,869 (**27%** churn rate)
* **Lost Total Revenue:** $2,862,927
* **Lost Monthly Revenue:** $139,131 (**31%** revenue churn)
* **ARPU vs. Churned ARPU:** $65 vs. $74

  ## Key Takeaways
* **Revenue Impact:** While overall customer churn is **27%**, revenue churn reaches **31%**, indicating that higher-spending customers are churning at a higher rate.
* **Contract Risk:** **Month-to-month** contracts show the highest churn rate (**43%**), compared to one-year (**11%**) and two-year (**3%**) contracts.
* **Early Tenure Vulnerability:** Churn is highest during the first **0–6 months** (**53%**) and steadily declines as customer tenure increases (reaching **10%** at **49+ months**).
* **Service & Payment Risk:** **Fiber optic** users paying via **Electronic check** experience the sharpest churn (**53%**). Automatic payment methods and longer contracts consistently correlate with lower churn.

## Recommended Actions
* **Convert Short-Term to Long-Term:** Offer targeted incentives or discounts to move month-to-month and fiber optic customers onto annual/two-year contracts.
* **Promote Auto-Pay:** Encourage switching from electronic/mailed checks to automatic bank transfer or credit card payments.
* **Strengthen Early Onboarding:** Focus retention check-ins, proactive support, and onboarding touchpoints on new customers within their first **0–6 months**.
